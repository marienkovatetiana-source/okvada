01

Scope

These terms apply to all road transport services provided by UAB Okvada. International carriage is governed by the CMR Convention.

02

Orders and quotes

A quote is valid for the period stated in it. An order is binding once confirmed by both parties in writing or by email.

03

Loading and unloading

The sender loads and secures the cargo unless agreed otherwise. Waiting time beyond the agreed 2-hour slot may be charged at €40 per hour.

04

Liability and insurance

Liability follows the CMR Convention. Cargo is covered by CMR insurance up to €300,000 per load.

05

Claims

Visible damage must be noted on the CMR at delivery. Hidden damage must be reported in writing within 7 days.

06

Prices and payment

Prices are net of VAT. Invoices are issued after delivery and payable within 30 days.

07

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Lithuania, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.
